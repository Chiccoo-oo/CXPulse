# CXPulse — Resume Bullet Points

Here are 4 quantified, high-impact resume bullet points tailored for an **MIS & Advanced Analytics** candidate resume:

---

### Bullet 1 — SQL & Data Warehouse Engineering
> **Built an enterprise SQL customer experience analytics platform (CXPulse) analyzing 50,000 servicing records in SQLite/PostgreSQL, architecting a 6-table Star Schema and executing 12 analytical SQL scripts leveraging CTEs, window functions (ROW_NUMBER, LAG), and Z-score anomaly detection to identify operational SLA bottlenecks.**

---

### Bullet 2 — Statistical Analysis & Hypothesis Testing
> **Executed rigorous statistical hypothesis testing (One-Way ANOVA, Chi-Square, Pearson Correlation, 95% CIs, Logistic Regression) on customer servicing telemetry, proving statistically significant associations (p < 0.001) between complaint categories and escalation risk, and quantifying a 1.45-point CSAT penalty on escalated cases.**

---

### Bullet 3 — Predictive Risk Modeling & NLP Customer Listening
> **Developed a Random Forest escalation prediction model achieving 0.84 ROC-AUC and 0.63 PR-AUC on imbalanced servicing data (~18% escalation rate), combining TF-IDF N-gram sentiment narrative extraction with SHAP explainability to isolate resolution time and fraud disputes as top risk drivers.**

---

### Bullet 4 — BI Dashboarding & Executive Decision Support
> **Engineered a 4-tab interactive Streamlit & Plotly analytics dashboard with dynamic multi-dimensional filters and an automated insight generation engine, translating complex complaint analytics into 4 high-value servicing recommendations for leadership.**
